Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

What are your delivery areas?

+

We currently deliver across major cities in India including Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Bangalore, Kolkata, Chennai, Hyderabad, Pune, and more. We're constantly expanding our delivery network.

How long does delivery take?

+

Orders are typically delivered within 2-3 business days for metro cities and 4-5 business days for other locations. All our products are made fresh to order.

What is your return policy?

+

We have a 100% satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy with your order, contact us within 24 hours of delivery for a full refund or replacement.

Do you offer bulk orders?

+

Yes! We cater to bulk orders for events, corporate gifting, and celebrations. Contact us at business@thekuaba.com for special bulk pricing.
